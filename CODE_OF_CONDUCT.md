# Code of Conduct

FocalDesk contributors and participants are expected to keep project spaces
welcoming, professional, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful when discussing design, bugs, users, and other contributors.
- Critique ideas and code with specific technical reasoning.
- Assume good intent while accepting corrections and new evidence.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or sustained
  disruption.

## Scope

This applies to FocalDesk repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions,
and project-related communication.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through an available
private GitHub contact channel. Do not use the security-reporting process for
ordinary disagreements or code-review disputes. Reports will be reviewed as
promptly and confidentially as the project's solo-maintainer status allows.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other
participation that violates these expectations and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.
