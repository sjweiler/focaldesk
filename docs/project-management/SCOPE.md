# Scope

## In scope

- The Wayland compositor, desktop shell, keyboard navigation, window management, and workspaces.
- Display, input, capture, and portal integration, including DRM/KMS and nested sessions.
- Desktop services, configuration, diagnostics, installation, and packaging maintained in this repository.
- Permissioned local automation, AI integration, and connectors within the documented capability and consent boundaries.

## Outside alpha scope

- Shipping a general-purpose Linux distribution.
- Providing complete desktop-feature parity or universal hardware and application compatibility.
- Guaranteeing stable interfaces or a release schedule during alpha.
- Granting automation broad access to desktop actions or sensitive data.

See the [project vision](../vision.md), [current project status](../../README.md#project-status), and [Change Management](CHANGE_MANAGEMENT.md).
