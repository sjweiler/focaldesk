# Retrospectives

Use this log after a release or a significant milestone. Link the relevant status report, release, or issue so follow-up work can be traced to the evidence. There are no retrospective entries yet.

## Entry template

- Date and milestone or release
- Expected outcome
- Observed outcome and supporting evidence
- What helped delivery
- What caused friction or escaped validation
- Follow-up action, owner, and tracking issue

Track outstanding actions in the linked issues and summarize their progress in [Status Reports](STATUS_REPORTS.md).
